About Me

I'm a final-year Electronics and Communication Engineering undergraduate at Aligarh Muslim University, with a CGPA of 8.964/10, placing me among the top 10% of my class. My core interest lies in analog and digital circuit design — I've worked hands-on with Op-Amps, LDOs, and bandgap references, and I'm currently exploring low-power, high-performance circuit techniques used in modern semiconductor systems.

Beyond the lab, I've contributed to two peer-reviewed publications, interned at the Semiconductor Laboratory (SCL) under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology, and led technical teams as Secretary of the EDIC Club and Joint Coordinator of AMURoboclub at ZHCET. I'm currently preparing applications for MS/PhD research positions and roles in analog/digital IC design.
